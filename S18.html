How we work · A busy or camera-shy founder

Founder content that doesn’t need Whitney all day

A founder-led strategy fails fast if it depends on Whitney being available and confident on camera every day. The system below is built around one focused hour, not constant presence.

1
One batch day a month
A single 3–4 hour shoot day banks 4–6 weeks of founder clips, cut down by the team afterward
2
Low-lift formats first
Voiceover over B-roll, text-led carousels in her tone, 10-second reaction clips — none need on-camera confidence
3
Ghostwritten, always approved
Captions and scripts are drafted in her voice from real conversations; Whitney only reviews, never writes from scratch
4
Cue cards, not scripts
In-salon moments run on 3-line prompt cards so real reactions can be captured in seconds, not rehearsed takes
